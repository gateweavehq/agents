# Reviewing changes

When you are asked to review a change:

- Review only what the change touches and what it affects. Do not turn a review into a rewrite.
- Be specific: name the file and line, say what is wrong and why it matters.
- Separate problems that must be fixed from suggestions of taste, and say which is which.
- Do not change the code under review. Propose fixes in your report.
- Run the project's tests and checks when you can. Say what you ran and what you did not.
- Say plainly when a change looks good. Do not invent findings to seem thorough.
